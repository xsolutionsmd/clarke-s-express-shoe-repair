# Company profile

Research observed **2026-10-17**. Confidence describes the evidence, not a guarantee of service availability. Source keys resolve in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Identity and match evidence

- **Verified:** Clarke's Express Shoe Repair, also publicly styled Clarke's Express Shoe & Leather Repair. Official domain: https://www.clarkesshoerepair.com/. The website, Yellow Pages and Google Maps match the Bel Air address and 410-836-8316. [S1][S12][S14]
- **Verified:** Bel Air shop is **23 South Main St. (REAR), Bel Air, MD 21014**, accessed through Burns Alley behind Boyd Fulford Pharmacy and The Main Street Tower Restaurant. Preserve REAR/Burns Alley in directions. [S1][S14][S16]
- **Verified address; conflicting legacy contact information:** Dundalk is **6920 Holabird Ave., Dundalk, MD 21222**; Google Maps labels the postal city Baltimore. The website and directory refer to the same shop. Current Google Maps and the explicit website notice publish **443-386-3800**; older pages/directories retain 410-284-2458. [S1][S4][S13][S15]
- **Verified account association:** the official site links Facebook `clarkesexpressshoerepair` and Instagram `clarke_the_shoe_doc`; both profiles match Bel Air location details. Facebook links back to the domain. Both also advertise `myshoeguy.com`; its redirect/ownership was not separately tested. [S1][S16][S17]
- **Single-source account link:** the official site links `https://twitter.com/ClarkeShoe`; activity and present ownership were not inspected. [S1]

## What they do and for whom

**Verified service categories:** shoe/boot resoling, heel repair, stretching, dyeing, handbag and leather repairs, zipper repair/replacement, baseball glove repair and orthopedic shoe lifts. The website and BBB list these services. Customers bring worn or damaged items for individual assessment; exact eligibility depends on the item. [S2][S3][S5][S20]

**Inferred visitor groups:** owners of everyday/work footwear, dress or vintage shoes, leather bags/garments and items with sentimental value; people needing fit modifications or repairs before an event. These groups follow the service descriptions, historical project captions and sampled reviews, not a demographic study. [S3][S7][S14][S15][S17]

## History and team

- **Verified founding year:** 1991, supported by the official site and BBB. The exact opening date is unresolved: BBB lists January 1; a 2011 Yellow Pages post says March. Use the year only. [S1][S12][S20]
- **Verified public owner identity:** James O'Farrell Clarke II, called Farrell or Jim, is named as owner/cobbler by the website; BBB names James Clarke as owner. “Master Craftsman” and “Shoe Doctor” are the business's own descriptions, not independently verified credentials. [S1][S6][S20]
- **Single-source:** Bryan Clarke is identified as Farrell's son and Senior Customer Service Specialist. Old owner-letter descriptions of his weekly schedule and years helping are historical, not a current staffing timetable. [S1][S6]
- **Historical/single-source:** a May 12, 2011 Yellow Pages announcement says Clarke's opened the Bel Air location in January 2011, formerly Sam's Shoe Repair. Do not confuse the premises' much older cobbler history with Clarke's 1991 founding. [S12]
- **Single-source:** the About page claims SSIA membership since 1983. Current membership was not checked with SSIA. [S2]

## Location and service area

**Current first-party operational statement:** Dundalk visits are by appointment, arranged by call/text; all repair work is performed at the main Bel Air shop. Google Maps corroborates the new contact number but does not independently confirm the repair-work arrangement. [S1][S4][S15]

The About page describes service to Baltimore and Harford Counties and surrounding communities. This is a local shop catchment, not evidence of delivery, collection or mail-in service. No supported service radius or shipping program was found in the inspected pages. [S2][S4][S5]
