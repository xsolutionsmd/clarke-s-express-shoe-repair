## Tone

The business describes personal craftsmanship, repairing valued possessions, honest estimates and direct customer communication. “Shoe Doctor” and “We Do It Right The First Time” are established self-descriptions. **Inferred writing direction:** approachable, specific and practical; explain options and limitations without inflated promises. [S1][S2][S6][S17]

Use concrete repair categories and the founding year. Avoid turning self-praise into independently verified claims: the site's “#1” wording and “Voted Baltimore's Best Cobbler” lack a verified voting body/year. The historical CBS inclusion is a different, dated fact. Do not imply authorization by the footwear brands shown in past projects. [S1][S7][S21]

Avoid invented medical credentials, guaranteed restoration, fixed turnaround, 24/7 emergency availability and unsupported price claims. Keep the current appointment/location distinction clear. [S1][S3][S5][S11]
