About the shop

“We do it right the first time.”

Clarke’s Express has repaired shoes and leather goods since 1991. Worn soles, tired leather, stubborn zippers and favourite bags are looked at carefully, explained plainly and repaired in the Bel Air workshop.

How a request works

Send photos

Tell us about the item and add up to five photos: the whole piece and a close-up of the area that needs work. No account needed.

We reply with an estimate

We look at your photos and reply with an estimate, or ask to see the item in person. The final price is confirmed when we see it.

Drop off and collect

Bring it to Bel Air during opening hours, or call or text to arrange a Dundalk visit. Every repair is done in the Bel Air shop.
